# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

CalEasy contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and
inclusive. Feedback should address the work rather than the person. Harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not
welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification before making assumptions.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and opinions.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing,
sexualized language or imagery, and publishing private information without
permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
repository's available private contact or security-reporting channel. Please
include relevant links, dates, and context. Maintainers may remove comments,
close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the
community safe.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and when someone is representing
the CalEasy community.
